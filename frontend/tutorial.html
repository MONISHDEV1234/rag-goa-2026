How to use SONAR?

A quick 4-step walkthrough for speaking queries, observing live stage state transitions, inspecting retrieved knowledge nodes, and monitoring latency telemetry.

01

Voice Recording & Quick Queries

Click the central Microphone Button to speak your query in English or Indian languages. Click again to stop. Alternatively, you can type your query in the terminal input bar or select any suggestion chip (RAG, FAISS, CHUNKING, LATENCY).

02

Real-Time Neural Pipeline Tracking

Watch the HUD status ticker progress live through:
STT Transcribing → FAISS Searching → Groq LLM Generating → Grounding Verification.

03

Grounded Responses & Source Citations

Review the factual answer with its ✓ Grounded badge. Inspect each cited passage, chunk strategy used, and vector cosine similarity score. Tap SPEAK for Web Speech synthesis audio playback.

04

Telemetry & Offline Demo Mode

Check the Latency Breakdown table comparing per-stage times against our <200ms budget. Tap SYS in the top header to toggle offline Demo Mode or customize Top-K retrieval parameters.
