What is SONAR?

SONAR — SOund Neural Answer Retrieval — is an ultra-low latency, voice-first Retrieval-Augmented Generation system engineered for HH Goa 2026. It seamlessly combines multilingual Indian-accent speech transcription with in-memory dense vector search and Groq LPU acceleration to deliver factual, cited answers in under 200ms.
